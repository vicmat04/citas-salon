# Sync Report: phase3-services-and-schedules

## Synced Capabilities

- **Service Categories & Catalog:** Dynamic category CRUD, service CRUD, price & duration configuration, default 10-minute buffer time, and active toggle.
- **Specialist Service Mapping:** Owner UI and Server Action to assign specific services to specialists.
- **Business Operating Hours:** Salon opening/closing hours per day of week.
- **Specialist Schedules & Blocked Shifts:** Specialist custom hours, inheritance from salon business hours, full-day date blocking (`BlockedDate`), and time-slot blocking (`BlockedSlot`).
